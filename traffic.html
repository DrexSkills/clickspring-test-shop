Lumen

Visitor simulator

Each button makes you a brand-new visitor arriving from that place. Then click around the site like a real person: read pricing, leave, or sign up.

Ideas to test ClickSpring

  1. Arrive from TikTok 3 times and leave right away → see “leave without clicking” in Why They Leave.
  2. Arrive from Google, open Pricing, then sign up → you become a customer in Visitor Journeys.
  3. Arrive from Instagram, open Pricing, then close the tab → Pricing shows up as an exit page.
  4. Try it on your phone too → ClickSpring compares phones vs computers.
  5. Made a tracking link in ClickSpring? Paste it in your browser to test campaign tracking.
  6. Add ?cs_debug=1 to any page to see the green “ClickSpring ✓” badge.

Your test data shows up in ClickSpring under the website “Lumen (test site)”. Switch to it from the sidebar or Settings.
